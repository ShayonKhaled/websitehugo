## Overview

A Discord bot that scrapes cafeteria menus and makes them easy to check without opening multiple pages.

## Problem

I wanted a quick way to check cafeteria meals from chat without manually browsing the university site every time.

## What I built

- A menu scraper that parses cafeteria listings
- A command-based Discord interface
- Lightweight formatting for daily menu output
